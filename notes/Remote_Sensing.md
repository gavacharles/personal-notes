- [The Technical Debt of Earth Embedding Products](https://cloudnativegeo.org/blog/2026/02/the-technical-debt-of-earth-embedding-products/): Isaac Corley (Feb 2026) compares Earth [[Embeddings]] products (AlphaEarth, Clay, Tessera, Presto, Major TOM and others) and argues that distribution is the real problem. He calls for cloud-native formats (GeoParquet, COG, Zarr), embedded CRS and provenance, and int8 quantization.
