- [agribound](https://github.com/montimaj/agribound): [[Python]] package and CLI that delineates agricultural field boundaries from [[Remote_Sensing]] imagery (Sentinel-2, Landsat, NAIP). It uses [[Deep_Learning]] models (Delineate-Anything, FTW, Prithvi, DINOv3) or [[Embeddings]] clustering and exports GeoParquet. `field-boundaries` `segmentation` `sentinel-2` `geoai` `earth-engine` `python`
