- [Satellite LST Is Not Air Temperature (TC Chakraborty, LinkedIn)](https://www.linkedin.com/feed/update/urn:li:activity:7478690057026207745/): Warns that satellite land surface temperature (LST) maps exaggerate heat hazard and the cooling effect of trees, since LST is surface 'skin' temperature, not the air people feel. Ships as a single Parquet file under CC BY 4.0. `floods` `disasters` `news-mining` `parquet` `open-data` `hazards`
